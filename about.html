Our Story

From 2016 to a Global Placement Partner.

ARK Global Connect commenced operations in 2016. In 2019, the company underwent a rebranding with new principles and ethics — sharpening our focus on trust, transparency and genuine care for the people we place.

We specialise in overseas placement and recruitment, with deep expertise in hospitality and general workers, overseas internship programmes, cruise ship jobs, overseas education consultancy, work permits, permanent residency, visa processing and travel assistance.

Today we connect skilled talent with employers across the Gulf, Malaysia, Australia and beyond — helping businesses find the right candidate for the right job, and helping candidates take the next step in a successful career.
