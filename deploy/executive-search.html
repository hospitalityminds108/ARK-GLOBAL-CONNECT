Our Method

How a Leadership Search Runs.

01

Immersion

We meet the board or hiring executive to understand strategy, culture, challenges and the profile of leader who will thrive.

02

Market Mapping

We map the entire relevant market — competitors, adjacencies and emerging talent — to build a complete picture.

03

Discreet Outreach

Personal, confidential approaches to candidates who are not actively looking.

04

Assessment

In-depth interviews and, where appropriate, psychometric and reference-based assessment of leadership capability.

05

Shortlist & Presentation

A small, high-quality shortlist with detailed dossiers and our recommendation.

06

Offer & Onboarding

Negotiation support, stakeholder alignment and onboarding guidance for a strong start.
