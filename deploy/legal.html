Recruitment Fraud Alert

Important: ARK Global Connect will never ask a candidate for money at any stage of the recruitment process.

Fraudsters sometimes impersonate recruitment companies to extract fees or personal data. Protect yourself by knowing the warning signs.

Warning Signs

  • Requests for payment for interviews, visas, training, or "processing".
  • Offers made without any formal interview or verification.
  • Communication from free email domains rather than our official address.
  • Pressure to act immediately or share sensitive documents.

How to Verify

Always confirm communication through our official channels: email arkglobalhm@gmail.com or phone +91 85913 52276. If in doubt, contact us before responding to any suspicious message.

Report It

If you believe you have been targeted, report it to us and to your local authorities. Your report helps protect others.
