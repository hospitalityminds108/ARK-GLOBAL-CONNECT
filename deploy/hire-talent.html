Our Process

A Search You Can Follow, Step by Step.

Transparency at every stage. You always know where your search stands and what happens next.

01

Understand

We take a detailed brief — the role, the outcomes, the team, the culture and the non-negotiables. We challenge assumptions and agree what success looks like.

02

Search

We map the market, activate our networks and talent pool, and run targeted outreach to reach both active and passive candidates.

03

Screen

Structured interviews assess skills, motivation, culture fit and salary expectations — so only genuinely relevant profiles reach you.

04

Shortlist

You receive a concise, well-documented shortlist with our assessment notes and a clear recommendation for each candidate.

05

Coordinate

We schedule interviews, manage candidate communication and keep both sides aligned throughout the process.

06

Offer

We support offer negotiation, manage counter-offers and help you present a compelling, competitive package.

07

Join

We stay engaged through notice periods and onboarding to protect the placement and ensure a strong start.

Replacement Guarantee

If a placed candidate leaves within the agreed guarantee period, we re-run the search at no additional recruitment fee. Terms are confirmed in writing before we begin.
